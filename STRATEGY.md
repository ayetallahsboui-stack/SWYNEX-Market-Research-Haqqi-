# Strategy Outline: Haqqi (SWYNEX)

**Author:** Sboui (SWYNEX Business & Marketing Strategy Intern)  
**Document Type:** Business & Marketing Strategy Outline

## 1. Positioning

**"Haqqi: Justice for Every Tunisian, With or Without a Smartphone."**

Haqqi is the first **inclusive LegalTech & GovTech bridge** in Tunisia designed for the digitally excluded. Unlike existing platforms (Juridoc, E-Tafakna, Ligalo) that target legal professionals, businesses, and literate users, Haqqi positions itself as a **voice-first, low-tech public service** that transforms complex legal and administrative procedures into accessible guidance via USSD (*123#) and Speech-to-Text in Derja.

| Segment | Current Alternatives | Haqqi's Positioning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **B2C (Vulnerable Citizens)** | Nothing (excluded from Idaraty) | **Voice & USSD-first**, no reading/smartphone required |
| **B2B (Lawyers/NGOs)** | Manual lead generation | **Qualified, pre-screened case files** in digital format |
| **B2G/CSR (Municipalities/Banks)** | Fragmented social programs | **Measurable inclusion impact** & CSR-ready reporting |

## 2. Channels

Haqqi’s go-to-market relies on **trusted intermediaries** and **low-tech access points**, not paid digital ads.

**B2C Acquisition (Bottom of the Pyramid):**
- **USSD Partnership:** Negotiate a free public-interest short code with Tunisie Telecom, Ooredoo, and Orange under the new 2026 USSD regulation framework (free for public services).
- **NGO & Municipal Desks:** Deploy "Haqqi Assistants" (trained staff) in municipality offices and partner NGO branches to guide citizens through the USSD/voice flow.
- **Mobile Caravans:** Partner with existing digital literacy caravans (similar to Tunisie Telecom’s rural tour model) to demonstrate the service in interior governorates.
- **Radio & Local Media:** Use community radio (Arabic/Derja) for awareness, as it reaches illiterate and rural populations effectively.

**B2B & Institutional Acquisition:**
- **Lawyer Referral Network:** Onboard 50–100 lawyers/notaries in pilot regions who receive pre-qualified leads in exchange for a success-based fee.
- **HiiL & Startup Tunisia Ecosystem:** Leverage HiiL’s justice innovation network and Startup Tunisia’s partner programs for credibility and pilot introductions.
- **CSR Pitches to Banks:** Target micro-finance institutions and banks seeking CSR impact metrics for financial inclusion.

## 3. Offer

**Tiered, Freemium Model (B2C + B2B):**

| Tier | Target | What They Get | Price |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Free (USSD/Voice)** | Vulnerable citizens | Basic legal orientation, procedure checklists, and referral to a lawyer/NGO. | **Free** (subsidized by CSR/B2G) |
| **Assisted (NGO/Municipality)** | Citizens via intermediaries | Guided case intake, document checklist, appointment booking with a lawyer. | **Freemium** (NGO pays per assisted case) |
| **Professional (B2B)** | Lawyers & Notaries | Pre-screened, digitized case files with client consent, reducing intake time. | **Success fee** per qualified lead or monthly subscription |
| **Impact (B2G/CSR)** | Municipalities, Banks | Dashboards on inclusion metrics, number of citizens served, and legal needs mapping. | **Annual license/contract** |

**Core Value Proposition by Segment:**
- **For Citizens:** "Get legal help without reading or typing—just call or dial *123#."
- **For Lawyers:** "Receive ready-to-review cases from underserved areas, expanding your client base."
- **For Sponsors:** "Fulfill your CSR mandate with verifiable, high-impact digital inclusion."

## 4. 90-Day Priorities (Pilot Phase)

**Days 1–30: Validate & Secure Partnerships**
- **Legal & Regulatory Check:** Confirm USSD free public-interest code eligibility with the Ministry of Communication Technologies and mobile operators.
- **Pilot MoU:** Sign a pilot agreement with **one municipality** (e.g., in Kairouan or Jendouba) and **one NGO** to host Haqqi Assistants.
- **Lawyer Network:** Recruit an initial roster of **20 lawyers** in the pilot region for referral partnerships.
- **Tech MVP:** Finalize a basic USSD flow (menu in Derja) and a voice call-back prototype for the pilot.

**Days 31–60: Deploy Pilot & Gather Data**
- **Launch Pilot:** Go live in the pilot municipality and NGO branches. Target **500 citizens** reached via USSD/assisted intake.
- **B2B Onboarding:** Onboard the 20 lawyers and test the pre-screened case file handoff.
- **Data Collection:** Track completion rates, top legal needs, and drop-off points in the USSD journey.
- **Ecosystem Visibility:** Present Haqqi at a HiiL or Startup Tunisia event to attract B2G/CSR attention.

**Days 61–90: Iterate & Prepare Scale-Up**
- **Iterate Offering:** Refine the USSD menu and lawyer handoff based on pilot feedback.
- **Secure First CSR/B2G Contract:** Convert pilot municipality or a bank CSR department into a **paid impact license** or subsidized service agreement.
- **Apply for Startup Label:** Submit application to the Startup Act labeling committee to unlock tax exemptions and grant eligibility (AIR: up to 30k TND; AIR2: up to 200k TND).
- **Prepare Scale-Up Plan:** Document pilot results (citizens served, cases referred, inclusion impact) for investor and partner conversations.
