# SWYNEX Market Research: Haqqi

**Author:** Ayatallah (SWYNEX Business & Marketing Strategy Intern)  
**Note:** This market research is conducted for a proposed pre-launch service (Haqqi) to validate market fit and user needs.

## 1. Target Customer Definition (Customer Profile)
*   **B2C (Primary Market):** The digitally excluded and vulnerable citizens in Tunisia. This includes:
    *   Illiterate individuals (Representing **17.3%** of the Tunisian population aged 10 and above, according to the 2024 National Census).
    *   Elderly populations lacking digital skills.
    *   Rural citizens without internet access (Digital divide is evident: internet usage is **81.4%** in urban areas vs. **61%** in rural areas).
    *   *Persona Example:* "Mariem, 45, from a rural area, cannot read and lacks a smartphone, but needs legal guidance regarding property or labor disputes."
*   **B2B (Secondary Market):** Legal professionals (Lawyers, Notaries), Psychologists, and NGOs who require qualified leads and pre-prepared digital case files.
*   **B2G/Sponsors:** Municipalities and financial institutions (Banks) looking to fulfill Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) while offering micro-finance services.

## 2. Market Size Indicators (Tunisia Context)
*   **Population:** ~11.9 Million (2024 Census).
*   **Literacy & Gender Gap:** The national illiteracy rate is **17.3%**. There is a significant gender gap, reaching **22.4%** among females compared to **12%** among males, highlighting the need for inclusive, voice-first solutions.
*   **Digital Divide:** Computer usage stands at **40.3%** in urban areas vs. only **21.2%** in rural areas. While overall internet penetration is 76.5% (World Bank, 2024), a massive segment of the population remains digitally excluded.
*   **Legal Sector:** Thousands of legal professionals and a high backlog of civil and labor disputes annually. 
*   **Addressable Market:** Millions of citizens needing accessible legal/administrative guidance (especially women and rural populations), supported by a B2B network of legal practitioners.
 
## 3. Key Trends
*   **GovTech & Digital Inclusion:** The market is shifting towards "Zero-UI" (Voice-first) interfaces to include marginalized groups who cannot read or type.
*   **AI & Local Language (Derja):** High demand for AI that understands local dialects (Derja) via Speech-to-Text, bridging the gap between technology and citizens.
*   **Low-Tech Accessibility (USSD):** Despite the rise of 4G, there is still a massive need for USSD (*123#) solutions for those without smartphones or internet.
*   **LegalTech Growth:** Increasing global and regional (MENA) interest in legal-tech platforms that connect users with lawyers efficiently.

## 4. Public Sources Used
*   **Tunisian National Institute of Statistics (INS) - 2024 General Census:** Illiteracy rate (17.3%) and gender disparity.
*   **Tunisian National Institute of Statistics (INS) - 2024 Digital Access Data:** Urban vs. rural internet and computer usage.
*   **World Bank Data (2024):** Internet users in Tunisia (76.5% of the population).
*   **Startup Tunisia Ecosystem Reports:** GovTech & Digital Inclusion trends.
